# 📊 Executive Summary Dashboard – Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview
The **Executive Summary Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze and monitor business performance. It provides a clear overview of sales, customers, orders, profit, quantity, product performance, regional sales, and payment methods.

The dashboard helps business stakeholders understand key performance indicators (KPIs) and make data-driven decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze overall business sales and profitability.
- Monitor total customers and total orders.
- Compare sales performance across different regions.
- Identify top-performing products.
- Analyze sales by store type and payment mode.
- Provide interactive filtering for better decision-making.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI Desktop** – Dashboard development and visualization.
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation.
- **DAX** – Calculated measures and KPI calculations.
- **Data Visualization** – Charts, cards, slicers, and interactive reports.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Gross Sales
- Total Customers
- Total Orders
- Total Profit
- Total Quantity

### 2. Sales Analysis
- Net Sales by Month
- Net Sales by Region
- Net Sales by Top 5 Products

### 3. Business Performance Analysis
- Profit by Category
- Gross Sales by Store Type
- Net Sales by Payment Mode

### 4. Interactive Filters
The dashboard includes the following slicers:
- Year
- Month
- Region
- Category

Users can select different filters to explore business performance from different perspectives.
